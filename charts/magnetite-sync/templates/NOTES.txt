magnetite-sync {{ .Chart.AppVersion }}: {{ .Values.replicaCount }} pod(s) on your database.

Settings are read when a pod starts; after changing one:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout restart deployment/{{ include "magnetite-sync.fullname" . }}

Check it:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "magnetite-sync.fullname" . }} 8787:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl http://127.0.0.1:8787/v2/health

Magnetite reaches any server but localhost over https:// only. Whatever is in
front of the service must terminate TLS, pass the Host header on, take request
bodies of up to 22 MiB and stream them to the server, and limit requests per
client (the chart's README).
